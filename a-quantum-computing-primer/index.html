A Quantum Computing Primer

From the state of a single qubit to the cryptographic migration it forces — organized around one recurring idea: quantum algorithms write answers in phase, then convert them to amplitude before measurement can look.

|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + βeiφ|1⟩

notation used throughout — teal: amplitude · violet: phase · chapters numbered as basis states
